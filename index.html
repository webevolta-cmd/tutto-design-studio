The studio

Tutto means “everything” in Italian. The studio works that way.

Tutto Design Studio is a full-service architecture and interior design firm in Yerevan. It is led by designer Anahit Israyeli Baghdasaryan, who has more than 17 years of international practice across Armenia, Russia and Germany.

One team covers concept development, architectural planning, construction supervision, furniture sourcing and custom-made pieces. To keep quality consistent down to the details, the studio imports premium furniture, lighting and accessories from European and Chinese manufacturers.

In 2025 Tutto moved into a new space on Heratsi Street, with a creative office and a modern showroom for the studio’s aesthetic: clean lines, timeless elegance and functionality tailored to each client.

Services

One studio, from the first sketch to the last lamp.

The four disciplines in the studio’s name run as one process, so the façade, the rooms, the light and the furniture are designed together.

Vahagni house · façade design

  1. Architecture

    Concept development and architectural planning for private houses, with façades drawn from the studio’s own sketches. The house in Vahagni has 4.2-metre ceilings and tall windows that bring daylight deep into the rooms.

    Concept · Planning · Façades

  2. Interior design

    Space planning, materials and finishes for apartments, houses and commercial interiors such as boutiques and salons. Every room is shown as a photorealistic visualization before work begins, and key rooms often come in two options.

    Apartments · Houses · Retail · Salons

  3. Lighting

    Light planned together with the architecture: concealed lines, backlit stone and mirrors, and statement fixtures from Italian lighting houses such as Euroluce, Italamp and MM Lampadari.

    Concealed light · Fixtures · Scenes

  4. Furniture & import

    Premium furniture, lighting and accessories imported from European and Chinese manufacturers, alongside custom pieces drawn by the studio, from kitchens and built-in beds to glass partitions.

    Import · Custom-made · Showroom

  5. Construction supervision

    The studio follows the build on site, so the finished space matches the approved design.

    Site visits · Quality control · Handover

Yerevan · 2020 · Private house · Architecture and interiors

Private house, Vahagni

A house designed from the studio’s own architectural sketches. Ceilings of 4.2 metres and tall windows opening onto the terrace fill the rooms with daylight. A glass partition designed by Tutto separates the kitchen from the living room, and each of the three bedrooms has its own palette.

For the TV wall the studio drew two options. The client chose black glass with a fireplace, set in the same travertine as the façade, so the interior and exterior read as one.

Approach

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Listen

    It starts with how you live: your needs, your routines and what the space should feel like.

  2. 02

    Concept & planning

    Architectural and space planning, with materials, light and furniture chosen together. Key rooms often come in two options to compare.

  3. 03

    Visualize & decide

    You see each room as a photorealistic visualization. Big decisions are made in person; smaller approvals can happen remotely.

  4. 04

    Source & make

    Furniture and lighting are ordered through the studio’s import partners, and custom pieces are detailed for the makers.

  5. 05

    Supervise & hand over

    The studio supervises construction on site until the space is finished.

“We meet the client five to ten times to make the final decisions, and the rest of the time we agree on materials remotely, by email. Some clients never met us in person at all. They walked into an interior that was already finished. That is how we work, and we worked this way before the pandemic too.”

Anahit IsrayeliStudio lead · translated from Russian
