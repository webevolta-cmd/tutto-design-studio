## Verified facts used on the site

- **Name:** Tutto Design Studio (About page: "Tutto Design Studio is a full-service interior design and architecture firm"). Logo wordmark "…tutto — interior design & architecture".
- **What they do:** concept development, architectural planning, construction supervision, furniture sourcing and custom-made solutions; import of premium furniture, lighting and accessories from European and Chinese manufacturers (About page). Disciplines named on the site: architecture, interior design, lighting, furniture.
- **Positioning:** "From vision to structure" (site tagline, typo-corrected). Clean lines, timeless elegance, personalised functionality (About page).
- **Experience:** 15+ years (Instagram bio). Studio lead Anahit Israyeli Baghdasaryan — "an experienced designer with over 17 years of international practice across Armenia, Russia, and Germany" (About page).
- **2025:** moved into a new location with a creative office and a modern showroom (About page) — consistent with the Heratsi St listing.
- **Address:** Heratsi St 18, 1st floor, Kentron district, Yerevan, Armenia (2GIS). Postal code 0025 (Google Maps address result for 18 Heratsi St).
- **Phone:** +374 96 700 704 (2GIS and Instagram bio snippet agree).
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00; no Saturday/Sunday hours listed (2GIS, updated 2026‑10‑19).
- **Social:** Instagram @tutto_design_studio, Facebook /tuttodesignstudio, YouTube @TuttoDesignstudio (all linked from 2GIS); import account @tutto_import_ (Instagram bio snippet).
- **Projects** (tutto.am posts, with the studio's own descriptions, translated from Russian where needed): Private house in Vahagni (Yerevan, 2020, architecture + interiors; 4.2 m ceilings; two TV-wall options, client chose black glass + travertine); Apartment in Erebuni district (2020–2021); #77 Apartment on Orbeli Street (2019, completed, duplex with children's rooms); Byuzand apartment (2019, completed, Art Deco); Rental apartment in Moscow (2020, completed; "after the design project was realised the rent rose by more than 30%"); Aroyan Jewelry store (2019, Art Deco); Elmar Image Centre beauty salon (2019); #55 Apartment in Yerevan (2019, completed).
- **Working method quote** (Orbeli Street post, Russian, attributed to Anahit Israyeli): meets clients five to ten times for final decisions, agrees materials remotely; some clients first saw the interior when it was finished.
- **Brands in the catalogue** (logos uploaded to the tutto.am home page in 2025): Alpi, AM Classic, Asnaghi, Bizzotto, Calligaris, Cattelan Italia, Euroluce, Galimberti Nino, Gattoni, Giorgio Casa, IFT Office, Italamp, Jupiter Lighting, Masiero, MM Lampadari, Porada, the.artceram, Tonon. Shown as text only.
- **Brand colours:** logo teal #00A8A8, favicon teal #00C4B5; storefront in travertine with a teal neon sign.

## Conflicts and how they were resolved

- **Founder:** the About text says the studio was "founded by Anahit Israyeli", while the team list on the same page names "Founder — Israyel Levoni Baghdasaryan" and "Head of Company — Anahit Israyeli Baghdasaryan". The site avoids the word "founder" and describes Anahit Israyeli Baghdasaryan as the studio's lead designer / head.
- **Years:** 15+ years (Instagram) vs. 17+ years of the lead designer's practice (About). Both are stated, each attributed correctly.
- **Old contacts:** the 2021 Contacts page lists "Cascade, Yerevan", phones (+374) 93 389 2968 and (+7) 963 977 4347, email tuttostudios@gmail.com and Instagram @tutto_design_architecture. These are superseded by the 2026 2GIS listing and are **not** used. The email could not be confirmed as current, so no email is published.
- **Erebuni apartment year:** English text says 2021, Russian says 2020 → shown as 2020–2021.
- **Vahagni house zoning:** the English and Russian texts disagree on which side holds the living zone → the site does not mention left/right.

## Media provenance

- All project images are the studio's own design visualizations published on tutto.am (originals listed with source URLs in `media-src/sources.json`). The website labels them as visualizations, not photographs of finished spaces.
- **Hero:** generated with Higgsfield (Kling 4.0 image-to-video, 1080p, 24 fps, 6 s) from the studio's render of the Vahagni living room, then upscaled to 4K with Higgsfield's ByteDance upscaler (AIGC preset). The upscale sharpens edges; it does not add genuine new detail.
- **Before/after:** *Before* is the studio's real 2020 site photograph of the Vahagni house frame. *After* is an illustrative visualization generated with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5) from that photograph and the studio's own façade render for the same house, so the two views align. It is labelled on the site as a design visualization, not a photograph of the completed building.
